It happens more than it should, but it wasn't always like this.

Before it used to be long hours spent getting distracted with videogames and whenever I was away from playing anything I would spend the rest of my days thinking about it. Wondering things like when am I getting back home so I can play again, I guess those were the first signs of getting addicted over something that was not so rewarding after all.

Nowadays I carry my distractions with me in a portable device, I get notifications about new things I should be consuming... it gets so irritating.

Listening to artists music just to not get creative with my own melodies start to be the bread and butter, sometimes I wonder how is it even possible that I can still read music and play my flute.

---
At this point, I start to believe it is pretty much a tendency of looking for more similarities with others. Maybe that is the only way to feel normal and feel like whatever I was thinking in creative ways can be 'copied', 'done', and 'bought' from others. It's just cope and instant gratification for having something similar that does not necessarily fit my needs. I guess that it's not only about distractions but getting easily frustrated about not 'having it my way' (or whatever that one song is about).
