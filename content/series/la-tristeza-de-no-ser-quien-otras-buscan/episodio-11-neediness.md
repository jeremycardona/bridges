This one is the latest of neurotic conducts I have learned. Not from me, because I never felt neediness from a human except from my father figure.
I know that the absence of my father was one of the most difficult things I had to live with, it would have been easier if he's sleeping in death.

I don't see this in me, I never have. I can great things with the limitations I do have and the constraints I live with. This day, I had a strange feeling. It was neediness from something I had stuck in my head and I let myself out. It felt strange when I let it be known to someone that authentically did not care at all. It been in that place of saying things to the wrong people, but never felt that way before. It was not easy at all, and I made me more conscious about what I should keep secret to others.

At this point, I want to stop.
At this point, I don't feel like ever sharing anything I write again.

It's that feeling of finding somebody, anybody and realizing that giving all for nothing in return is truly a void. It causes a void when what's given is expected to return something, so by design it is not given from love. It's more than just the plain word, it is the value given to it. I have not seen anything beyond infinity that is greater than love.

That feeling of void made me think at this moment, that is was getting what I was giving. 
I had not been giving any love at all, and that is the worst parts of it.
