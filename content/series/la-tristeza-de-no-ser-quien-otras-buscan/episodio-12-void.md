It is beautiful to start with a blank new page.

It was that fear of trying over and over, after all those collapses...

I really know that I never had let any of the collapses well known with words, but there is really a massive confusion in my head from the past. There are moments that I think that the collapses were caused by the LSD, THC, DMT, psilocybin, and even sex. I was not planning on building anymore at those moments, I was really prepared for destruction and deception. To cause confusion and lies and at some point harm. I'm not trying to say that these causing things are bad, dangerous and destructive but that initiative I had for doing and trying them were.

Things that get burned down are for a reason. Rebuilding may require to pick up what is scattered and save what is worth.

Those bad moments really had strong lessons for me, having to notice the lessons after a long time and having to take a long time to recover from self-destruction. It is not self-realization that made start taking better decisions but the people around me that helped out in excellent ways...

So naturally I would say to give all the negativity to return nothing, to give negative to the void. This idea really does not make much sense, because I know how much self-improvement I have seen. There is still possible ways of taking the bad a getting good 'lessons' out of it. It has to be done from a rational decision, with extreme care that we are not fooled by our own deceptions.
