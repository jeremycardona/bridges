I visualize myself in a small island surrounded by the ocean, near nowhere, maybe where the only option is to write letters inside glass bottles and send them anywhere.

I know it's unrealistic, mostly it feels like a state of mind; most of the messages are written for my future self. After all it is possible to get somewhere. 

It's just that state of mind and I don't know. I really just find myself in this state every once in a while, and I don't know how to differentiate it from behaving like a lunatic.

Maybe in the modern world is much easier to get in this state where there is a reminder, a song, a video, a photo, an old conversation stored in the cloud. It's much deeper because it's almost like a rabbit hole that pulls back and every time that the brain is ready to forget we simply can't.

So, I don't have much words when it comes to this state anymore. I just find myself under the moonlight writing, drawing symbols, reading some of my favorite texts.

Drinking makes it much worse and worst because it enhances the deep feelings for whatever reason finds me in that very place. Listening to sad music is just another stimulant that I breathe like air. "The state is deep, so just be, I'll be; not in the place I wanna be, at least being here a while makes it less worse" and I just think that afterwards I'll be finding the future me sober from melancholy, elsewhere...
