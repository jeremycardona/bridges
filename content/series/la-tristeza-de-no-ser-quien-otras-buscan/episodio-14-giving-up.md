Giving up is not necessarily a wrong decision, and I would not be able to know when it's the right time to give up. While there is still time and there is possibility for getting something done it's likely that I will be occupied.

I have become quite fragile on the idea of giving up sooner than later. Some days I don't feel youthful at all, I simply don't feel a pull towards anything.

Everything becomes tiring, exhaustion feels constant it hardly wears out.

Incompleteness is another spiral
