For the longest of time it always has been my own self-esteem.
Those long walks staring at the floor constantly while passing people on the sidewalks.
Trying not to pay attention to noise and getting all the distractions that are bothering out of my mind.
Wondering about the things that other people are talking out loud as a natural 'high' and 'trip'.
Just being serious so I can get things done correctly.
Spending time doing long readings on short books just because I want to sub-vocalize every word so that the story comes to life.
Getting my time wasted on trying to solve hard problems that I know I am not yet prepared to solve.
Realizing that I am doing this series for self-love.

It's really boring doing all that if you really think about it. Why not spend some time traveling, going out with friends, shopping, gaming, watching streams? I guess that I get fear of having to take risks. I never feel to secure in this world; harm, war, violence, assaults, theft, I feel like that is all I hear from the radio, tv, and the internet.

Yeah, in that sense I been inside the bubble of utopia living in my tiny reality where I don't wanna see any of that happen in front of my eyes.
Lately, I have been preparing to watch more of that happen anywhere. So, I kinda had to get my self taking risks.

I know that at this point I am never going to be prepared for everything, but at least I want to have control over my own life, live taking more risks and responsibility.

Some of the things I have not been able to handle is facing the truth that 24 hours are sometimes not enough for having a good day. Instead I started to count a day as abstract numbers where I just stack 24+ hours and count it as an abstract day. In that sense, counting days started to be fun. I started to feel like more creative as well. Doing more,  not giving up on  my projects just because it has not been completed in 24 hours made me realize that 'natural highs' can be so much more. It keeps me motivated, but yeah... now I have to count twice as much (the standard in common with everybody else aka following the calendar, and those abstract days).

I have never felt so much passion for what I do, it scared me because I am not even doing anything of it yet. I feel like I have stayed on the lowest levels for the longest while and I am not able to climb up yet because I have not seen *an interested soul that loves those things that I do.*

It gets really hard to explain what I am even trying to do, it feels like a bunch of heuristics, a looot of frameworks, a lot of trial and errors. I have been absolute and down for some time, and that gave me a chance to try new things (and stuff).

I decided to keep thinking and rethinking some of the most important conversations and moments I have experienced while fighting intrusive and negative feelings. The hardest part of it was really not suppressing any of them, but getting to know them and understanding it's root cause. Those conversations had me feeling deep, and fighting a lot. I most likely can say I did not get fatigue and every iteration was worth the run.
