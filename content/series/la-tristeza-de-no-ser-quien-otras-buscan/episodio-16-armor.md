Armor simply covers the weakest parts of a knight.

The vulnerabilities I show the most are hidden under the cover of this armor. They are like weak spots in my body that are covered by an armor that does not help defending.

I see the vulnerabilities is the sound of my voice, when approaching, when avoiding, when looking who is left by my side.

It's really noticeable when looking my reflection and everything that is around. If there is anything it's that paranoid feeling of defenselessness. There is nothing better than just taking off all this armor that does not serve any purpose.

The armor gets exploited, it shows every weakness, it's vulnerability in disguise.

Sometimes I don't even know what the armor is for, but it does feels safer having to wear it until the war ends. My consciousness tells me that is fine to show cover than what's underneath. After all there are moments where the amor gets slightly better, my body gets stronger and defenses are unbreakable.
