It would be cool to say caffeine and alcohol have been the go to picks in this topic, but it's really not the case.

For some reasons I opt not to talk much about it, but it is true to say that reading and talking about this topic has always been surprising to me because of how different the experiences are.

After a couple of beers in it would be normal for me to think about how I never really use or make any use to the things I make. It's that melancholy that really hits every time.

After a couple of cups of coffee things get exciting for me, and no matter how bad my day is it helps me get over the worst parts. One, two or three cups sounds reasonable. I just don't think it's the caffeine alone that makes all the good parts even better.

For some time now, the experiences has not been the same any more. These are normalized substances we see every day and I don't think they are safe to use on a day to day basis. I feel like it's unreliable to get our own brains altered by chemical substances, even worse to abuse them and get addictions. I just don't see the right occasions to use them right, or a reason to have them accessible anywhere.

From my point of view, making use of them should only be safe when it's healthy. Even then I consider there are risks, since they are generally so mind altering. I think balance is the key and not extending it for long periods. People that are under psychoactives don't look normal.
