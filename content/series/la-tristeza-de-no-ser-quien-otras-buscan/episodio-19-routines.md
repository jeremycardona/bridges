It's not a routine what I need when the one thing I need to be doing is important enough to take most significant part of my day.

---
It's so unrealistic to have a routine that repeat the majority of our self existence. Yes; jobs, professions, journals, whatever. We are just not built to repeat for so long the same boring routine.

It starts to feel a lot like anxiety when there is more than we could have quietly done by ourselves, but did we have control over it?

Routines eventually become a burden and a boring activity for our own creativity. Schools, workplaces, home, etc proves this. We can be occupied on so many co-relating activities that keep us engaged and fulfilled, we are just preoccupied about an expectation.

It's a lot like a workout, but this time it's more extensive and it does not give a physical result such as an aesthetic appeal. We want to look good, but maybe not on the outer self. And what about those personalities that does not really care about anything physical? 

There is a lot to relate about recovery, growth, leadership... none of these really seem to intersect from a distance but there is a lot about human soul.
