I guess that cycles is something that repeats and that is just many different ways of describing iteration, loops, and recursion.

I know that they are different and sometimes they can be seen as the same from a very simple starting point. 

---
A lot of patterns that repeat and never seem to end are very beautiful. It's the most powerful cognitive recognition that exists.

Maybe it's a just another illusion of eternity. We don't know, but sure it is repetitive enough to deceive ourselves from the distinction of eternity vs repetition. Since for us as humans it's not imposible to loop ourselves in a routine, an instruction, a simple life. None of these things are bad and if they were it really depends.

It's just the idea of having something never ending that we are capable of controlling, it does not even have to relate to the domain of computer programming.

---
Eternity is not like a cycle, eternity might be scary, but it should not be.
